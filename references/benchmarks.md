# Calibration corpus — what the levels ARE

**Last updated: 2026-07-30.** (The kill-gate's freshness check flags this file when it's >6 months old.)

The judge's answer key for the height ladder. All entries are public, verifiable campaigns. **Usage: calibrating level judgment — NEVER as templates. A concept mimicking a corpus entry is by definition ≤6 ("new territory" cannot be borrowed).** Descriptions are *mechanism*, not execution — the mechanism is what's being learned.

## The structural truth
Static-carried awarded B2B is a THIN niche — Cannes B2B is dominated by stunts and film. Which means: the level-7+ ambition in static B2B competes on partly open ground, and calibration needs all three bands below — canon (8–10), modern midfield (5–7), and wallpaper (3–4).

## Band 9–10 — canon (static)

### The Economist — White out of Red (AMV BBDO, 1988→2020)
*"'I never read The Economist.' — Management trainee. Aged 42."* · *"In real life, the tortoise loses."*
**Mechanism:** the reader completes the idea; flatters the audience's intelligence; an asset system (white on red) so strong the logo is redundant — decades of series on one device. **Level 9–10.**
**Lesson:** insider wit + reader-completion + unmistakable format beats product argument — and is the cheapest thing to produce.

### Volkswagen — Think Small / Lemon (DDB, 1959–60)
**Mechanism:** owns the category's "weakness" with self-distance; empty space as scroll-stop; the self-accusation ("Lemon.") forces reading and makes the quality promise more credible than a claim. **Level 10.**
**Lesson:** the truth nobody wants to say is the strongest raw material; emptiness stops the eye better than a packed layout.

### JCDecaux — Meet Marina Prieto (DAVID Madrid, Creative B2B Grand Prix 2024)
An unknown 100-year-old's Instagram on 850 metro posters, no logo — the medium proving its own effect. **Level 10.**
**Lesson:** self-proving demonstration — use what you sell to prove what you sell; curiosity (senderlessness) as engine.

### Reporters Without Borders — the self-censoring ad (Åkestam Holst NoA, Guldägget nominee 2023)
A broadsheet page whose ink fades — the text censors itself in front of the reader. **Level 9.**
**Lesson:** the medium's physical properties can CARRY the message; a time dimension inside a static surface.

## Band 8–9 — awarded/canonical

- **Avis "We try harder"** (DDB 1962–63): the confession buys credibility for every promise. **9.**
- **Getty Images "Endless Possibilities"** (AlmapBBDO 2015): iconic portraits built as mosaics of stock images — the product demonstrated in the execution; two-step discovery (distance→closeness). **8–9.**
- **IBM "People for Smarter Cities"** (Ogilvy France, Outdoor GP 2013): posters folded into bench/rain shelter/ramp — the ad physically proves the message. **9.**
- **FT "No FT, no comment."** (1982): the social cost of standing answerless — the reader fills it in. **8.**
- **Mailchimp "Did You Mean Mailchimp?"** (Droga5 2017): misspelled near-names (FailChips, SnailPrimp…) across OOH + physical products; search behavior completes it. **9.**
- **Segment "Good morning, LA!"** (2019, San Francisco): the wrong city on the billboard = bad data demonstrated in the medium. **8.**

## Band 3–4 — wallpaper (negative anchors; real ad patterns)
The B2B SaaS category convention: "everything in one place" claims · UI/dashboard hero · before/after split · checklist-✅ bullets · promo CTA · testimonial quote + posed portrait · gated-guide collage · quiz lead-gen · thought-leadership monologue ("INSIGHT: …"). All are real, running ad patterns scored 3–4(5). **A judge giving these ≥6 is too kind — the discrimination control in the gauntlet exists for exactly this.**

## The mechanisms recurring in everything awarded (the judge's checklist)

1. **The reader/viewer completes the idea** — the point isn't delivered pre-chewed (Economist, Getty, Segment).
2. **A truth nobody said out loud** — about the audience, the category, or oneself (Think Small, Avis).
3. **Proof as demonstration in the medium itself** (Marina Prieto, IBM, Segment, RSF).
4. **Genuine artifact/reality over borrowed costume** — documentary observation and category-internal artifacts beat borrowed culture formats.
5. **Human + moment, not product + claim** — the Lemon traits recur in almost everything awarded.
6. **The asset system is built in from day 1** — the winners are series-able (Economist for decades; Ramp's Kevin universe).
